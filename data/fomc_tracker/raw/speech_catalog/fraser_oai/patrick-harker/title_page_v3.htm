    2015-2025
    AUTHOR:
    • Harker, Patrick T.
    CONTRIBUTING AUTHOR:

    ABSTRACT:

    This is a collection of transcripts of public statements of Patrick T. Harker. Harker served as President and Chief Executive Officer of the Federal Reserve Bank of Philadelphia from July 1, 2015 to June 30, 2025.
