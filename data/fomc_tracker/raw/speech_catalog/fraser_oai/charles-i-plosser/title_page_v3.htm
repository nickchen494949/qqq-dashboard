    Statements and Speeches of Charles I. Plosser

    2007-2015
    AUTHOR:
    • Plosser, Charles I.
    CONTRIBUTING AUTHOR:
    • Federal Reserve Bank of Philadelphia

    ABSTRACT: This is a collection of transcripts of public statements of Charles I. Plosser. Plosser served as president of the Federal Reserve Bank of Philadelphia from 2006-2015.
