    Statements and Speeches of Tom Barkin

    2018-2025
    AUTHOR:
    • Barkin, Thomas I.
    CONTRIBUTING AUTHOR:
    • Federal Reserve Bank of Richmond

    ABSTRACT:

    This is a collection of statements and speeches of Tom Barkin. Barkin took office as president of the Federal Reserve Bank of Richmond on January 1, 2018. His current speeches can be found at the Richmond Fed's website.
