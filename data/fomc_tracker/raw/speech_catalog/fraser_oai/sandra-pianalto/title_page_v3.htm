    Statements and Speeches of Sandra Pianalto

    2003-2014
    AUTHOR:
    • Pianalto, Sandra
    CONTRIBUTING AUTHOR:
    • Federal Reserve Bank of Cleveland

    ABSTRACT:

    This is a collection of transcripts of public statements of Sandra Pianalto. Pianalto served as president of the Federal Reserve Bank of Cleveland from February 1, 2003, until her retirement on May 31, 2014.
