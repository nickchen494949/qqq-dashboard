    Statements and Speeches of John C. Williams

    2011-2025
    AUTHOR:
    • Williams, John C. (John Carroll)

    ABSTRACT:

    This is a collection of transcripts of public statements of John C. Williams. Williams served as President of the Federal Reserve Bank of San Francisco from March 1, 2011 to June 17, 2018. He took office as President of the Federal Reserve Bank of New York on June 18, 2018. Williams's current speeches can be found at https://www.newyorkfed.org/aboutthefed/orgchart/williams
